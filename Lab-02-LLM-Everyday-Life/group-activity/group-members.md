## Problem statement
A typical university day can require a laptop, charger, phone, books, notebooks, stationery, water bottle, headphones, ID card and more. Carrying everything is uncomfortable and inconvenient. The group had to design a product that helps students carry, organise, or reduce what they take with them.

## Chosen solution
**LoadLight**, a smart backpack with a companion app (see [activity-outcomes.md](activity-outcomes.md)).
